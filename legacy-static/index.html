Brew guides

Starting points, not commandments. Taste, then tweak.

Pour over

1:16 · 94°C · 3:00

  1. Rinse the filter, discard the water.
  2. Bloom with 2× coffee weight for 30s.
  3. Pour in slow circles to full weight by 1:30.
  4. Let it drain. Swirl. Sip.

AeroPress

1:14 · 90°C · 2:00

  1. Inverted. Add coffee, pour to full.
  2. Stir 10 seconds, steep 90s.
  3. Flip onto the cup, press gently for 30s.
  4. Stop at the hiss.

French press

1:15 · 95°C · 4:00

  1. Coarse grind, pour all the water.
  2. Wait 4 minutes. Do not stir.
  3. Break the crust, skim the foam.
  4. Press slowly, serve immediately.

Cold brew

1:8 · fridge · 14h

  1. Coarse grind into a jar, add cold water.
  2. Stir, cover, refrigerate overnight.
  3. Strain through a paper filter.
  4. Dilute 1:1 with water or milk, over ice.
